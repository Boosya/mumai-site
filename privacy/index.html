Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

The short version

MumAI exists to lighten your mental load — not to profit from your data. You're trusting us with information about your family, and we take that seriously. Here's our promise in plain terms:

  • We collect only what we need or will need to make MumAI work for you. Nothing more.
  • We never sell your personal information. Ever.
  • Your data is never used to train AI models — not by us, and not by our AI provider.
  • We won't sell your data. No data brokers.
  • You can see, correct, or delete your data at any time.

The rest of this policy explains the details. If anything is unclear, email us at privacy@mumai.com.au — a real person will reply.

1. Who we are

MumAI is operated by Lemniscate Investments Pty Ltd [ABN: 74156435899], an Australian company ("MumAI", "we", "us"). This policy covers the MumAI app and our website at mumai.com.au (together, the "Service"), wherever in the world you use them.

If you're in the United Kingdom, we are the "controller" of your personal data under UK data protection law. If you're in the United States, we are the business responsible for your personal information under applicable state privacy laws.

2. What we collect

Data minimisation is a design rule at MumAI, not a slogan. If a feature doesn't need a piece of information, we don't ask for it — and we regularly review what we hold. Here is everything we collect.

Information you give us

Your details. When you sign up, you give us your name, and you may give us information about where you live. You can share other information about yourself when you use MumAI but we don't ask for any other details.

Your family's information. The details you choose to add so MumAI can actually help: family members' names and ages, schedules and calendar entries, school and activity details, tasks, notes, and the emails or messages you ask MumAI to help with. What goes in is always up to you.

Connected accounts. If you choose to connect an email account or calendar, we access that content only to provide the features you've asked for — like spotting a school event in a newsletter. Connecting is always optional, and you can disconnect at any time. If you connect a Google account, section 6 sets out our specific commitments.

Information collected automatically

Usage and device information. Basic technical data such as device type, app version, IP address, and how features are used. We use this to keep MumAI secure, fix bugs, and understand what's helpful. We keep analytics to a minimum and never use them to build advertising profiles.

Sensitive information

Some of what you add may be sensitive — a child's allergy, a medical appointment, family circumstances. You're never required to add sensitive information to use MumAI. When you do, we rely on your consent to handle it, we use it only to provide the Service to you, and it gets every protection in this policy.

3. What we don't do

Just as important as what we collect is what we don't:

  • We don't sell or rent your personal information — to anyone, for any reason.
  • We don't use your data to train AI models, and our AI provider is contractually prohibited from doing so too.
  • We don't sell any data. We believe your data is yours and should never be sold.
  • We don't collect information from your children or family members. Only you use MumAI, and only you decide what goes in.

4. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and personalise MumAI — this is the heart of the product: remembering your family's context so our help is genuinely useful, not generic.
  • Communicate with you about your account.
  • Keep the Service secure, prevent fraud, and fix problems.
  • Understand usage and aggregated insights that cannot identify you or your family.
  • Meet our legal obligations.

For users in the UK, our legal bases are: performing our contract with you (providing the Service), your consent (sensitive information, connected accounts, marketing), our legitimate interests (security, fraud prevention, service improvement), and compliance with legal obligations.

5. How AI works with your data

MumAI's intelligence is provided by custom built code from the MumAI team and Anthropic, an AI company. When you use MumAI, for features that use AI, the information needed to help you, plus relevant family context, is sent securely to Anthropic to generate a response.

Anthropic processes your data only to provide the Service and is not permitted to use it to train its AI models.

MumAI never takes an action on your behalf — like sending a message or an email — without your approval.

Your account is isolated: your family's information is never mixed with, or visible to, any other account unless you give express permission — such as sharing information with a parent, co-parent or additional carer.

If we ever change or add an AI provider, we will hold them to these same standards and notify you before the change takes effect.

6. If you connect a Google account

This section applies if you choose to connect your Gmail or Google Calendar to MumAI. MumAI's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

What we access. We request only the narrowest Google permissions needed for the features you actually use, and we access your Gmail messages and Calendar events only to provide those features — for example, spotting a school excursion in an email or checking your calendar for a clash.

How we use it. Google user data is used only to provide or improve user-facing features that are visible to you in MumAI. It is never used for advertising, and it is never used to train AI or machine-learning models — not generalised models, not our own, and not Anthropic's.

How we store it. We store only what a feature needs — for example, an event MumAI has extracted for you — not a copy of your mailbox. We do at times store parts of the information you share with MumAI to ensure the quality of the AI suggestions can be verified by you as true and correct.

How we share it. Google user data is transferred only to Anthropic, and only as needed to generate the response you've requested — for example, summarising an email you've asked about. It is never transferred to advertisers, data brokers, or any other third party, and it is never sold.

Human access. No one at MumAI reads your Google data, except with your explicit permission (for example, when you ask us to help with a support issue), where necessary for security investigations, or where required by law.

You can disconnect your Google account at any time in MumAI's settings, or from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Disconnecting stops our access immediately.

7. Who we share information with

MumAI app works directly on your device and all information is shared locally. We share personal information only where necessary to run MumAI advanced AI features. It is shared only with providers bound by contract to protect it and use it solely to provide services to us:

  • Anthropic — AI processing, as described in section 5.
  • Apple — secure storage of any MumAI feedback you send via the Apple MumAI app.
  • Professional advisers and authorities — only where the law requires it (for example, a valid court order). We will push back on requests that are too broad, and tell you about requests for your data where the law allows us to.

While you may pay for MumAI, all payments are managed through Apple and this information is not shared with MumAI.

If MumAI is ever part of a merger or acquisition, your information will remain protected by the commitments in this policy, and we will notify you before any change takes effect.

That's the whole list. No advertisers, no data brokers, no undisclosed "partners".

8. Your family's information and children

MumAI is built for one person: you. You must be 18 or older to create an account, and you are the only person who interacts with MumAI. MumAI may sync information with calendars that you explicitly connect with and these calendars may be people other than you. Your children, family members, and other contacts never interact directly with MumAI, never have accounts or profiles, and are never contacted by us.

When you add information about your children, you're trusting us with it as their parent or guardian. We treat that information with the same care and protections as everything else in this policy, we use it only to help you, and you can edit or delete it at any time.

9. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored locally on your device, and data that needs to be shared for the purpose of delivering the service to you, is stored on secure cloud servers located in Australia, and may be shared to servers in the United States. When you use some AI features, relevant information is processed by Anthropic in the United States.

Wherever your data goes, our protections travel with it. For transfers out of Australia, we take reasonable steps required under Australian Privacy Principle 8 to ensure recipients handle your data consistently with Australian privacy law. For transfers out of the UK, we use lawful transfer mechanisms such as the UK's international data transfer agreement or addendum. In every case, our providers are contractually required to protect your data to the standard of this policy.

10. Security

All MumAI data is stored explicitly on your device and we protect your information with encryption in transit, strict access controls, and account isolation — your family's data is walled off from every other account. Our providers are required to maintain equivalent safeguards.

No online service can promise perfect security, and we won't pretend otherwise. What we can promise: if a data breach ever puts your information at risk, we will tell you promptly and honestly, and we will comply with the notifiable data breach requirements in Australia, the UK, and applicable US states.

11. How long we keep your data — and how to delete it

We keep your information while your account is active, because that history is what makes MumAI useful to you. You can delete individual items in the app at any time.

If you delete your account, we delete your personal information within 120 days, except for the small amount we're legally required to keep (for example, payment records for tax purposes), which we keep only as long as the law requires. Account deletion is permanent — your tasks, calendar, notes, and family information cannot be recovered.

12. Your rights

These rights are yours wherever you live. You can:

  • Access a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct anything that's inaccurate.
  • Delete your information or your entire account.
  • Request an export of your data in a portable format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time (for example, disconnecting a linked account or unsubscribing from marketing).

To exercise any of these, email privacy@mumai.com.au. We'll respond within 30 days, we don't charge for reasonable requests, and we will never treat you worse for exercising your rights. We may need to verify your identity first — that's to protect your data, not to slow you down.

If you're in Australia

We handle your personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles. If you have a concern, contact us first and we'll do our best to resolve it. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

If you're in the United Kingdom

Under UK GDPR you also have the rights to restrict or object to certain processing, and not to be subject to solely automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects — MumAI makes no such decisions, and never acts without your approval. You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk), though we'd appreciate the chance to resolve things with you first.

If you're in the United States

Depending on your state, you may have rights to know, access, correct, and delete your personal information, to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, and to appeal if we decline a request. We don't sell your personal information or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising — so there is nothing to opt out of — but we honour Global Privacy Control signals regardless. We will never discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

13. Cookies and analytics

Our website may use essential cookies (needed for the site to work) and minimal, privacy-respecting analytics via CloudFlare. We do not use advertising cookies or cross-site tracking. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

14. Changes to this policy

If we make a material change to this policy, we will communicate with you before it takes effect — we won't rely on you happening to re-read this page. The current version, with its date, will always be available at mumai.com.au/privacy.

15. Contact us

Questions, requests, or concerns about privacy: privacy@mumai.com.au
