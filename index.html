The problem

The mental load
is real.

A newsletter PDF arrives by email. A follow-up lands in the class WhatsApp. The reply deadline is buried three pages into the PDF. And somewhere in all of that, your kid needs you to RSVP to a birthday party, order a gift, and work out who's driving.

None of it is hard. There's just so much of it, spread across so many apps, arriving at the worst possible moments. Things slip — not because you're disorganised, but because the system is broken.

MumAI is a quiet assistant that sits on your phone and helps you stay on top of the operational side of family life — without taking over, and without asking you to change how you already work.

What it does

From buried inbox
to done, in a few taps.

📧

School emails, surfaced

Point MumAI at any sender — school, sports club, music teacher. It pulls the latest email, including text from any PDFs attached or linked inside.

💬

WhatsApp in one tap

Forward any message from your class groups into MumAI using the iOS share sheet. No screenshots, no copying and pasting.

📅

Act on it immediately

Draft a reply, add an event to your calendar, set a reminder, or fire back a WhatsApp — all from one place, with the right context already there.

🔒

You stay in control

MumAI never sends a message or saves an event without you tapping approve. Every suggestion waits for your say-so. Always.

How we think about it

AI that helps,
not hijacks.

A lot of AI tools want to do things for you. MumAI's approach is different: it handles the boring, time-wasting part — finding the thing, pulling the context, drafting the message — and then hands control back to you before anything actually happens.

Your emails, your calendar, your family's WhatsApp messages — these are personal. MumAI lives on your device, keeps your data private, and only ever suggests. You decide what gets sent, saved, or ignored.

No autonomous sends. No calendar writes without your approval. No surprises.
