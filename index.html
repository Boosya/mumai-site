How we think about it

AI that helps,
not hijacks.

A lot of AI tools want to do things for you. Mum.ai's approach is different: it handles the boring, time-wasting part — finding the thing, pulling the context, drafting the message — and then hands control back to you before anything actually happens.

Your emails, your calendar, your family's messages are personal. Mum.ai lives on your device, keeps your data private, and only ever suggests. You decide what gets sent, saved, or ignored.

No autonomous sends. No calendar writes without your approval. No surprises.
