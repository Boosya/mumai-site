The problem

The mental load
is real.

A school newsletter PDF arrives by email. A follow-up lands in the class WhatsApp. The reply deadline is buried three pages into the PDF. Somewhere in all that, your kid needs you to RSVP to a birthday party, order a gift, and work out who's driving.
